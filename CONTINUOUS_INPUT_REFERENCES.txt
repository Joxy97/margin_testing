Canonical reduction: Q_ii=-w_i; Q_ij=P on original graph NONedges, otherwise 0;

penalty 2P=3576876, greater than any vertex reward. Removing an endpoint of a
violated pair strictly improves the objective. Thus every minimum is a clique
and min E is exactly minus the maximum ORIGINAL WEIGHTED clique weight.
There are 44,997,890 nonzero off-diagonal pairs, not 4,997,110: actual QUBO
density is 90.0047804780478%. Integer objective arithmetic is int64-safe and
its absolute coefficient bound is below 2^53; author binary64 weight sums are
also exact integers for this input.
